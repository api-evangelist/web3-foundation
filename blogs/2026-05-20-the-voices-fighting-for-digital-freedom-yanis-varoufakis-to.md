As Europe’s debate over privacy, surveillance, and digital sovereignty intensifies, Web3 Summit 2026 brings Yanis Varoufakis, Amir Taaki, Gavin Wood, and other rebel voices to Berlin to confront one urgent question: who should control our digital future? Web3 has never been only about code, blockchain infrastructure, or tokens. Its essence has always been about power: who holds it, who profits from it, and whether the people can reclaim any meaningful control over their digital lives.
