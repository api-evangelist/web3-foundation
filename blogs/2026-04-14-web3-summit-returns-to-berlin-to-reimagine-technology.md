More than a tech conference, Web3 Summit returns to Berlin as a collaborative festival where builders, artists, and cultural thinkers come together to participate in and shape usable, human-centered Web3. Web3 Summit returns to Funkhaus Berlin on June 18–19 with a vision that reaches beyond the standard tech conference. More than a gathering for builders, it is a participatory festival that brings together builders, artists, technologists, and thinkers to explore a more human-centered digital future — one for people who believe technology should serve human autonomy, not extract it.
