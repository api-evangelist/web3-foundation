Germany’s opposition to Chat Control highlights growing concerns about surveillance, encryption, privacy, and the future of online digital freedom. The debate over the future of the internet is no longer confined to technologists, policymakers, or privacy activists. It is becoming a mainstream political and societal question: what kind of digital systems should people be expected to live under?
