Today, the Web3 Foundation (W3F) is entering a new chapter, one that is defined by focus, purpose, and a deliberate return to our founding mission: stewarding and accelerating the technologies that make decentralized web possible at a global scale. Over the years, W3F played a highly active, hands-on role in helping bootstrap networks such as Polkadot and Kusama. This phase was instrumental in bringing these networks to life and supporting the early growth of their communities, governance, and technical foundations.
